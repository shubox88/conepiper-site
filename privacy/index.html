Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 13, 2026

ConePiper is an AI-powered sales execution software product operated by Paper Wrench ("ConePiper", "Paper Wrench", "we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use ConePiper and its integrations.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect:

  • Account information such as your name and email address.
  • Business and workspace information you provide to ConePiper.
  • Information obtained through services that you choose to connect to ConePiper.
  • Technical information such as logs, browser information, IP address, and security events.
  • Usage information relating to how ConePiper features are used.

2. LinkedIn Integration

If you choose to connect your LinkedIn account, ConePiper may receive information made available through LinkedIn's authorized APIs and the permissions you approve.

This may include, depending on the permissions granted:

  • Your LinkedIn member identifier.
  • Your name, profile information, and email address where permitted.
  • Information required to perform LinkedIn actions that you explicitly authorize.
  • Authentication tokens required to maintain the authorized connection.

ConePiper will only request LinkedIn permissions that are required for supported features. We do not obtain your LinkedIn password.

Where ConePiper supports publishing or other LinkedIn actions, such actions are performed only through LinkedIn's authorized APIs and subject to the permissions granted by the user.

3. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Provide and operate ConePiper.
  • Authenticate users and maintain connected integrations.
  • Analyze sales and pipeline information provided by users.
  • Generate recommendations and assist with authorized workflow actions.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot errors.
  • Improve the reliability and functionality of the product.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. Third-Party Services

ConePiper may integrate with third-party services selected by users, including LinkedIn and other business software platforms.

Information processed by those services is also subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

ConePiper is not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn Corporation. LinkedIn is a trademark of LinkedIn Corporation and its affiliates.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information.

We may share information with service providers that help us operate ConePiper, such as hosting, infrastructure, authentication, monitoring, and other technology providers, only where necessary to provide the service.

We may also disclose information when required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect users, Paper Wrench, or the security of the service.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security and audit records, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

Authentication credentials and integration tokens are retained only as necessary to maintain integrations authorized by the user.

7. Disconnecting LinkedIn

Users may disconnect their LinkedIn integration from ConePiper when that feature is available. Users may also revoke application access through their LinkedIn account settings.

After access is revoked, ConePiper will no longer be able to use the revoked LinkedIn authorization.
